For judges · about 5 minutes

Test it as a real group

  1. Start a plan on the home screen (or “Try with a sample group” to start with 3 sample friends). No sign-up.
  2. Make your planet: name, food, interests, your busy times, wishes, private budget.
  3. In “Your crew”, click Open as a second person ↗. A new tab opens as a different person. Join there with different times and a different budget.
  4. Back in the first tab the crew and shared free time update live. Click Bring everyone together, drag the planets in, and see 3 plans on the real Manhattan map.
  5. Vote and comment in both tabs. The creator locks in the top plan and books (simulated).
  6. Come back: open Me → My plans in the header. Your plans, friends and outings are saved, your planet is pre-filled next time, and “Remind me in 4 weeks” adds a calendar reminder.
  7. Paywall: click “Ask AI to organize”, create a second plan, or open Subscribe. Pick an Event Pass, Plus, Family or Crew. Pay with Stripe test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, then the AI organizer runs on Claude.
